## Published MCP tools (6)
- `cloutdesk__list_collabs` — enumerate an agency's collaborations (scope `collaborations:read`)
- `cloutdesk__get_collab` — read a collaboration's deliverables, locked terms, payment schedule, commission split, metrics (scope `collaborations:read`)
- `cloutdesk__get_talent_profile` — read a creator's audience demographics and performance baseline (scope `creators:read`)
- `cloutdesk__get_revenue_summary` — read AR aging, invoice state, payout queue with Stripe Connect context (scope `transactions:read`)
- `cloutdesk__emit_event` — append an event to a collaboration timeline / audit trail (scope `events:emit`, write)
- `cloutdesk__comment_on_deliverable` — post a coordination note on a deliverable (write)

Input schemas are not published and `tools/list` cannot be called; only names, purposes, and the argument names shown in the provider's own JSON-RPC examples (`status`, `collab_id`, `creator_id`, `deliverable_id`, `body`) are known.
